Available now

HisabApp Offline

Designed for small to medium businesses that want one user and one authoritative device without depending on a continuous internet connection.

  • Complete POS, accounting, inventory, purchasing, and reporting
  • One designated device remains the source of truth for the business
  • The same business may use additional devices, but the designated device remains authoritative and synchronizes the official dataset to the cloud
  • This controlled model avoids conflicting records and reconciliation problems
  • Multilingual documents, backup, restore, and supported thermal printing
Staged development

HisabApp Online

Designed for medium to large businesses that need connected operations across people, locations, and devices.

  • Complete POS, accounting, inventory, purchasing, and reporting
  • Owner, admin, manager, sales, purchasing, and customer roles
  • Multi-user and multi-device access with online synchronization
  • Server-authoritative records with permission-aware workflows
  • Customer access and hosted digital-store options
