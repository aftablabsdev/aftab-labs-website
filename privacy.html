Information you share

If you contact Aftab Labs by email, WhatsApp, or the website contact form, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, and message.

We use contact information to respond to enquiries, discuss projects, provide support, and maintain our business communications. When you submit the contact form, we also send a confirmation to the email address you provide. We do not sell personal information.

The contact form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to help prevent spam and Brevo to deliver the enquiry and confirmation emails. These providers may process limited technical and contact information as needed to provide those services.

Third-party links

This website links to services including Google Play, product demonstrations, and WhatsApp. Those services have their own privacy practices and policies.

The site is primarily static and hosted through Cloudflare Pages. Hosting and security logs may be processed by the hosting provider as needed to operate and protect the service.
