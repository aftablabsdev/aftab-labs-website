Modulora ERP

Pre-release • Android and web testing

Connect the modules your business needs—without losing control.

Modulora is a cloud-first, multi-tenant ERP for service, retail, trading, contracting, and light-manufacturing businesses. Teams work from one authoritative system with role-based access and modular capabilities.

Finalized product identity

The product is Modulora ERP.

The approved modular M surrounds a central cube, representing one ERP core connecting every operational module. The identity uses Modulora deep navy, teal, cobalt, and warm white.

The Android release candidate has passed broad Phase 9 QA, and the rebranded web client is deployed for continued acceptance testing. Modulora remains pre-release while browser acceptance, account management, role behavior, recovery, and multi-device sessions are validated.

Modular by design

One operational core, configured around the business.

Modules can be enabled according to the workflows a team needs, while historical data remains protected when a module is disabled.

Sales & invoicing

Quotations, invoices, cash, card, credit sales, payments, and controlled reversals.

Customers & receivables

Customer accounts, statements, aging, payments, and credit controls.

Jobs & approvals

Work orders, assignments, materials, progress, documents, and approval stages.

Inventory & purchasing

Stock receiving, FIFO movement, returns, valuation, purchase orders, and suppliers.

Cash, bank & expenses

Balances, transfers, settlements, expense categories, movement history, and reports.

People & payroll

Employees, compensation, commissions, payroll runs, deductions, and advances.

Manufacturing

Versioned bills of materials, production batches, consumption, outputs, and costing.

Documents & permissions

Private attachments, generated documents, tenant isolation, roles, and permission overrides.

Built for controlled collaboration

Shared data without shared authority.

Modulora separates businesses, memberships, employees, and permissions so each user sees and performs only the work assigned to them.

Multi-business

Users can belong to and switch between authorized businesses.

Role-aware

Owner, Admin, Manager, Accountant, Staff, and Viewer responsibilities remain distinct.

Server-authoritative

Financial, stock, approval, and permission rules are enforced beyond the interface.

Auditable

Important workflows use approval, void, reversal, and history rather than silent deletion.
