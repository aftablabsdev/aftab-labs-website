Nexora ERP

Planning and feasibility

A planned managed ERP for the operational depth of growing UAE businesses.

Nexora is a separate Aftab Labs product direction for a broad, web-first business suite. It is currently being shaped through architecture, accounting, localization, licensing, security, and pilot feasibility work.

Status matters

Nexora is not yet a released product. Its scope remains subject to feasibility and release gates.

Current planning update

Feasible—with deliberate release gates.

The recommended direction is a commercially managed ERP based on a pinned, evaluated open-source foundation, extended through clearly separated Aftab Labs product modules and operations.

Before an MVP can move forward, the selected stack must pass accounting and UAE localization validation, software licensing review, security and restore testing, and a controlled pilot definition.

Proposed MVP

Broad business workflows, introduced responsibly.

The first target is a UAE SME in trading, wholesale, distribution, or professional services with a focused operational team.

CRM & sales

Leads, activities, quotations, sales orders, pricing, and customer relationships.

Purchase & suppliers

Requests, purchase orders, receipts, supplier bills, and payments.

Inventory

Products, warehouses, locations, transfers, adjustments, and traceability where required.

Accounting foundation

Ledgers, journals, invoices, bills, taxes, payments, and reports—only after validation.

UAE localization

Chart of accounts, VAT configuration, and business documents reviewed for the target use.

Users & roles

Clear operational roles, access controls, activities, and auditable administration.

Managed operations

Deployment, monitoring, encrypted backups, restore procedures, and product support.

Responsive web

A web-first experience before any separate native mobile-product commitment.

Go / no-go gates

Trust must be proven before launch.

Licensing

Every platform component, dependency, modification, and distribution duty must be approved.

Accounting

A UAE accountant must reconcile and accept the required financial workflows and reports.

Security

Hardening, access review, monitoring, backup, and full restore evidence must pass.

Pilot

A controlled SME pilot must complete critical workflows without manual database intervention.

A distinct ERP direction

Nexora and Modulora serve different product strategies.

Modulora is Aftab Labs’ independently developed, cloud-first modular ERP currently in testing. Nexora is a separate planned managed ERP direction built around a broader evaluated platform foundation.
