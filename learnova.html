Learnova

Under development

Learn. Practice. Master. Grow with confidence.

Learnova is a curriculum-aligned learning platform designed to make progress visible and practice purposeful. It will begin with a focused Nursery and Early Years experience, then expand grade by grade.

The learning loop
Learn → see examplesIntroduce a clear objective through age-appropriate guidance.
Practice → diagnose → masterUse evidence to guide support, review, and progress.
First release focus

A safe, measurable, game-like Early Years learning experience with useful parent visibility.

Current development focus

Building the foundation before expanding the curriculum.

The current phase is defining a narrow Early Years curriculum slice, learning objectives, diagnostic evidence, mastery rules, safety and privacy requirements, and measurable pilot outcomes.

Student experience prototypes, parent reporting, structured content authoring, and curriculum crosswalk work will be validated before a broader product release.

Product principles

A learning journey that matures with the learner.

Learnova is being designed around educational clarity, child safety, and evidence of understanding—not simply time spent on screen.

Curriculum aligned

Structured objectives connected to carefully defined British and American curriculum pathways.

Mastery focused

Practice, diagnostics, review, and progress are connected through explainable evidence.

Age adaptive

A game-like Early Years experience that can mature into more independent study over time.

Parent visibility

Clear summaries of strengths, support areas, progress, and recommended next activities.

Accessible by design

Large touch targets, alternatives to audio, readable contrast, captions, and reduced motion where needed.

Safe foundations

Child privacy, consent, content quality, security, and responsible analytics are release gates.

Content governance

Structured authoring, review, approval, publishing, rollback, and material change history.

Measured pilots

Small controlled pilots will shape expansion before new grades and curriculum slices are added.

Planned progression

Start focused. Expand with evidence.

Foundation

Curriculum slice, experience prototype, measurement, safety, privacy, and release criteria.

Early Years MVP

Student learning loop, parent dashboard, content studio, and a controlled closed pilot.

Primary expansion

Add validated subjects and grades while preserving the shared mastery architecture.

Secondary pathway

Progress toward Grade 12 with mature study, revision, and assessment experiences.
